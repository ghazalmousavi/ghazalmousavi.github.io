About ME

I'm Ghazal 👋
I graduated with a bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering (Communications) from the University of Tehran.

Currently, I work as a remote research assistant at the University of Alberta, under the supervision of Prof. Masoud Ardakani. My research focuses on Beyond Diagonal Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface (BD-RIS)-aided communication systems.

I also work at the Signal Processing & Communication Systems Laboratory at the University of Tehran with Prof. Ali Olfat. My work there focuses on detection algorithms for decentralized massive MIMO uplink systems, with a focus on learning-based approaches.

I'm interested in massive MIMO, RIS-aided communications, and signal processing.
Feel free to take a look around and check out my research and publications.
